About the Fund
What Is It?

The Joe Espy SGA Scholarship Fund is a need-based emergency scholarship administered by the University of Alabama's Student Government Association. It exists to provide immediate financial relief to UA students facing unexpected hardship — the kind of sudden crisis that can derail an entire semester or worse.

Unlike traditional scholarships that reward merit months in advance, the Espy Fund is utility-based — meaning funds help students cover real-life needs like rent, car payments, utilities, and other essentials outside of academic obligations. Students in crisis can apply and receive assistance quickly, because some situations simply can't wait.

"Students in need of essential financial assistance due to a temporary hardship or unexpected emergency are encouraged to apply for a need-based scholarship award from the Joe Espy SGA Scholarship Fund." — UA Student Care & Well-Being

The fund is available to both full-time and part-time UA students throughout the academic term, with applications opening after classes begin each semester.

Who It Helps
Who Can Apply?

Any enrolled UA student facing a documented, sudden financial emergency is eligible. The kinds of hardships covered include:

  • Sudden loss of income or family financial support
  • Unexpected medical or mental health expenses
  • Housing or food insecurity caused by an unforeseen event
  • Natural disasters affecting a student's primary residence
  • Death in the immediate family accompanied by financial hardship
  • Any other documented crisis placing a student in temporary financial distress

Each application is reviewed individually by an SGA committee — the goal is to make sure students get the actual help they need, quickly.

Why It Matters
Your Impact

College is hard enough under normal circumstances. But for students who are already stretched thin, one unexpected event — a medical bill, a job loss, a family emergency — can be the difference between staying enrolled and dropping out entirely.

The Joe Espy Fund exists to be that lifeline. When you sponsor Battle of the Bands, you're not just buying wristbands and putting your chapter's name in lights. You're directly contributing to a fund that keeps fellow Crimson Tide students in school and on track to graduate.

Whether you are a student org, a local business, or a community member — this is your chance to give back and be part of something special.
